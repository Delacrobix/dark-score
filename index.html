Convert your sheet music to dark mode

Reading sheet music on screen at night, or on a dark stage, with the traditional white background is exhausting for your eyes. Dark Score intelligently inverts the colors of your PDFs and images: it removes scan noise, thickens staff lines, and applies the color scheme you prefer.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload

    Drop a PDF, PNG or JPG. Multi-page PDFs and several files at once are supported.

  2. 2

    Adjust

    Pick a preset for your situation, then fine-tune contrast, scan cleaning and line thickness with a live preview.

  3. 3

    Download

    Export a PDF or PNGs with the dark colors baked in. They open in any reader: forScore, GoodReader, tablets, laptops.

Your scores never leave your device

We understand your scores are important to you. That is why this site never copies or sends your files to any external server: all processing happens directly in your browser. The only things that leave your device are anonymous, cookieless usage statistics and crash reports, never your files or their contents.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dark Score free?
Yes. It is free and open source (MIT), with no account and no limits. If it helps you, you can support it on Ko-fi.
Are my scores uploaded anywhere?
No. Your files are processed inside your browser using Web Workers and never reach a server. The app keeps working offline once loaded. We only collect anonymous, cookieless usage statistics (which pages are visited, which presets are used) and crash reports.
Which files can I convert?
PDF (including multi-page), PNG and JPG, up to 50 MB each. You can drop several files at once and export them merged or separately.
Does it work with scanned or photographed scores?
Yes. The scan cleaning control removes yellowed paper, shadows and noise, and line thickening restores staff lines that get lost when inverting.
Can I read the result in forScore, GoodReader or on a tablet?
Yes. The export is a regular PDF or PNG with the dark colors baked in, so it looks the same in any reader and on any device.
Why not just invert colors in my PDF reader?
A plain inversion turns scan noise into white blotches, thins the lines and gives a grey, muddy result. Dark Score cleans the page, thickens lines and maps the colors to palettes designed for reading in the dark.
